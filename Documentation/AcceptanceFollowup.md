# Acceptance follow-up

The supplied playtest tally was 36 passed, one failed and 26 N/A. The individual report was unavailable in the checkout. This checklist covers the reported overlap and named deferred checks. Keep each manual result open until a tester records the checkpoint, scene, input device and observation.

1. **Target indicators and menus.** In Edit Mode select **Tools > Windrunner > Acceptance > Play traversal and rewards lane**. Click Game. Aim at the three Air Nodes straight ahead, then the Sling enemy on the right. With a reticle visible, open Pause (Escape), Settings, Save game, Load game and Dagger Arsenal. Back out one level at a time. For Journal, open a disposable Zone 01 playthrough and press J while aiming at a basin Sling enemy. Neither reticle nor its release flash should cover a menu. After the last menu closes, only a valid target should return. For the lost-target case, pause, disable **Acceptance targets** in the runtime Hierarchy, then resume. Expect no old reticle. Re-enable the group and aim again. Stop Play; relaunch the lane for a full reset.
2. **Cast, Sling and interruptions.** On the lane aim at the center Air Node. Hold/release Cast (default left mouse) outside the perfect window and again inside it. Watch projectile, return stream, main meter and feedback. Chain casts across the other two nodes while the first recovers. Face right, hold/release Sling (default right mouse) early, then in the perfect window. Compare targeting, charge, travel, impact and launch to the production behavior. F5 returns the player to the deck. During charge or travel press F6 to remove targets; press again to restore. Repeat with pause during charge. A stale lock, phantom hit/reward, frozen player or stuck reticle is a failure.
3. **Reserve, transfer and HUD.** Equip Farseer's Daggers in Arsenal (default I), close Arsenal and let the main meter fill. The compact Second Wind meter should show its own colored fill and current/50. F8 delivers four controlled 5-point rewards through the production reward entry point; this bypasses projectile travel only for the burst exercise. Check 20, 40, then 50 reserve at full main. Further rewards must cap at 50. At full main, activation (default R) must leave the reserve intact. F7 spends one quarter of main capacity. Activate immediately; only missing main energy returns and the entire reserve empties. Try again at zero. Repeat using real node returns, including when the main meter has room. Swap to Training Daggers: the reserve meter should disappear. Stop and relaunch for fresh resources. The lane uses a memory playthrough and redirects slot operations to a new temporary directory.
4. **Independent saves.** Use the lane's disposable pause menu, choose Save game > Save an independent copy, and create two copies. Compare their slot codes in Load game. Load one, change equipment, save it and load the other. Check the second has its own equipment and progress. The normal game lists the world, completed quest count, short slot code and current slot. Similar names and timestamps can belong to different playthroughs.
5. **Timed retrieval expiry and retry.** Open **Window > General > Test Runner > PlayMode**. Search for and run **ZoneChaptersPersistWhileInterruptedDeliveryRestartsAtSource**. The isolated playthrough reaches the actual ridge quest, unloads the world while carrying, reloads, collects again, lets the timer expire, attempts an invalid turn-in and retries after respawn. Expect one successful delivery and no expiry reward. Expiry discards the attempt and temporarily hides the reusable source model. To judge the presentation manually, accept Ridge Retrieval in a disposable Zone 01 playthrough, pick up the object, wait for the countdown to expire, return to its source after respawn, collect it and reach the giver before expiry. Check the expiry notice and successful delivery.
6. **Swimming and waterfall.** Open **Assets/AirflowPrototype/Generated/Batch1_Playground.unity**, enter Play, and follow the authored shore into deep water. Check entry, ascent (Space), descent (Left Ctrl), pause/resume and exit to shore. Use **Tools > Windrunner > Acceptance > Select waterfall sources** to locate the waterfall; move the actual player near it and then beyond 125 units at a fixed listening volume. Expect localized near ambience, a much quieter distant tail and no increase after reload. Scene-view camera motion alone does not move the listener.
7. **Terrain authoring.** Save a separate scene copy of Zone 01. On the copy select its Terrain, open **Tools > Windrunner > Terrain Studio**, choose **Basin surfaces**, then **Attach editable working copy**. Verify Terrain and TerrainCollider point to the new TerrainData before painting. In Project open **Assets/WindrunnerTerrain/Presets** for Meadow, Earth, Broken stone, Wheel wear and the road presets. In **Terrain Materials**, inspect the profile’s grass, soil and rock slots. Make a small stroke on the working copy and undo. Check the surface, shading, collider and original TerrainData. See TerrainStudio.md for the multi-layer and road checklist.
8. **Movement Gym.** Open **Tools > Windrunner > Movement Lab > Open Movement Gym**, enter Play, choose a Testing section, then **Teleport to selected section**. Follow the lane markers for Wind Tunnels and Overdrive. Use **Reset measurements** between attempts. Record steering, acceleration, exit momentum and any failed finish separately from the automated station results.

The Air Node availability reported in the Zone 01 route was a content-coverage gap. The traversal lane uses the StartingWorld production player, node components, resource service and HUD. Its deck, controls and cloned targets exist only during explicitly launched Editor sessions.
