# Progression fixture and journal acceptance

Implemented from `5761575b` on `feature/progression-framework`. This is a targeted usability/integration correction, not a redesign of quests, relationships or movement. The accepted playground, character configuration, portable animation, abilities and authored progression IDs are unchanged.

## Findings and corrections

- Interaction queried the first camera-ray collider during Update, before the orbit camera's LateUpdate. The player's own collider or an unrelated volume trigger could conceal a valid station. The controller now probes after camera movement, ignores only the bound actor's hierarchy and non-interactive triggers, chooses the nearest remaining hit and fails closed on query saturation. Solid occluders, actor distance, layers, channels and prerequisites still apply. Tests exercise an opaque wall, trigger, range rejection, missing unlock and rebound input.
- `ProgressionActor` and `ProgressionHost` already resolve the registry-owned service and surviving input host on reload. That ownership is preserved. The fixture now accesses the surviving input host's existing pause controller for its visible pause/binding panel; it does not introduce another pause state. Previously the fixture's null pause view made Escape appear to freeze the game without a menu.
- The old journal was an unstructured text panel without mouse cursor ownership or sufficient navigation bindings. It now has status-grouped navigation, story/challenge categories, selected-versus-tracked indicators, objective counters/bars, guidance, relationships, cinematic actions and independently scrolling details. Keyboard/gamepad navigation uses the existing central action asset. Shared, nested cursor requests prevent journal/pause closure from restoring a stale cursor state.
- The fixture now has named, colored stations, different beacon shapes, visible checkpoint/finish arches, route markers and nearby camera-facing labels. Five engineering station positions were spread out for sightlines; no race gate, interaction range, physics component or production gameplay tuning changed. All 108 original scene objects/components remain. Existing MonoBehaviour configuration is unchanged except display prompts and serialization of an already-existing empty unlock field.
- The field guide observes actual service snapshots and race/puzzle sessions. F2 controls provide scoped pause/cursor ownership, reload, confirmed isolated reset, cinematic interruption and separate diagnostics from the last unpaused authoritative probe. Reset requires the exact scene, player and engineering slot; the runtime entry also verifies the registered service identity. No progress is awarded by the guide.
- The engineering marker Timeline lasts eight seconds so play/skip/interruption can be inspected. It is still placeholder content, not a final cinematic. No story, animation or audio assets were invented.

## In-Unity manual guide

Open `Assets/WindrunnerProduction/Progression/Fixtures/ProgressionFixture.unity`, press Play and focus the Game view. No extra scene setup is required. The on-screen labels show current bindings; defaults below are only a reference. F1/right-stick click collapses the guide; F2/left-stick click opens fixture controls and releases the cursor. Nearby signs appear as you approach to prevent distant labels stacking together.

1. **Fresh or restored state.** Read the guide's quest/rank/puzzle state. To test fresh, open fixture controls, choose **Reset Fixture Progress**, then explicitly confirm. Only `progression-engineering-fixture.json` is replaced. Its previous contents become the normal rolling `.bak`, which later saves replace. Choose **Reload saved fixture** to preserve progress instead. Failure: normal player saves change, duplicate roots appear, or the fresh state retains ranks/completions.
2. **Both offers and prompts.** Collapse the guide if it covers a station. Walk toward blue **QUEST OFFER A**, aim the center reticle at its collider and press the displayed Interact binding (E/gamepad west). Repeat at violet **QUEST OFFER B**. Both become Active. Stand farther away: the prompt should explain the range restriction and input must not accept the quest. In controls, diagnostics show the last targeted object, binding, measured distance/range and rejection reason. Failure: no prompt at a visible in-range station, acceptance through a wall, or accepting twice.
3. **Journal and focus.** Open with J/gamepad Select. Click a quest or use up/down/D-pad. The arrow/highlight marks selection; **TRACKED** separately identifies tracking. T/gamepad south toggles tracking for an active quest. Left/right or triggers switch categories. Mouse wheel or Page Up/Down/shoulders scroll details. Backspace/gamepad east closes. While open, movement and camera input must not move the player. Press Escape to layer the visible pause menu, resume it, then close the journal: gameplay resumes only after both requests close. Failure: stuck input/time/cursor, inaccessible entries, tracking silently changing selection, or selected details belonging to a hidden category.
4. **Rebinding.** Escape opens the fixture pause/binding panel. Click the desired Interact or journal binding and supply a new control; Backspace cancels a pending rebind. Resume, aim at a usable station and verify the prompt and actual input both use the new control. Reopen the panel to restore your preferred binding. These are the normal shared input preferences and intentionally persist; the fixture progress reset does not reset them. Hardware mouse/rebind ergonomics remain a manual acceptance check.
5. **Shared world objective.** Use the gold **WORLD SWITCH** after accepting both quests. Both journal counters become 1/1; B becomes ready, while A still needs the race. Selection/tracking must not restrict the other quest's progress. Failure: only the tracked quest updates or the shared switch awards a relationship rank before turn-in.
6. **Race.** Interact with cyan **RACE START**. Follow the route through the cyan **RACE CHECKPOINT**, then the green **RACE FINISH**. The guide reports the actual attempt/time/checkpoint. A should become Ready to turn in. Failure: walking past only the finish completes the ordered race, or a normal successful run never updates A.
7. **Independent turn-ins.** Beyond the finish, use blue **QUEST TURN-IN A** and violet **QUEST TURN-IN B**. Each matching character advances to rank 1 exactly once. Both quests move to Completed and their cinematic entries unlock. Failure: one turn-in advances both relationships, repeat use grants another rank, or completed chapters remain active.
8. **Cinematic recovery.** Stop moving on the floor, open the journal and scroll to its cinematic buttons. Play one entry and let the eight-second marker Timeline finish. Play the other and use Enter/gamepad south to skip. Replay an entry, open F2 controls and click **Interrupt cinematic**, then Resume exploration. Camera/input/time must recover; an interrupted unresolved entry stays pending, while replay interruption preserves its prior resolution. Replays grant no rank/reward. The engineering Timeline is a control-lifecycle fixture, not a finished visual sequence. Failure: stuck camera, paused movement after closing controls, or false watched credit on early interruption.
9. **Puzzle.** Approach the orange **PUZZLE SWITCH 1** and **2** on the right. Use 2 first to inspect wrong-order recovery, then 1 followed by 2 to solve. The guide shows the real puzzle state. A permanent solution survives reload; use the confirmed fresh-fixture reset to repeat from scratch. Failure: wrong order counts as solved or a solved puzzle re-awards its first-clear entitlement.
10. **Persistence and lifecycle.** After completing the flow, open F2 controls and reload the saved fixture. Inspect both ranks, completed quests, cinematic records and solved puzzle. Exit and re-enter Play Mode to verify disk restoration. Repeat journal open/close, pause/resume and scene reload. Failure: duplicate notifications/listeners, missing durable progress, duplicate grants or stranded controls. Automated scene tests use an isolated test store; this exact button-to-disk workflow remains manual acceptance.
11. **Visual/accessibility acceptance.** In the Game view resolution selector test 1280×720, 1600×900 and 1920×1080. Expand/collapse the guide; inspect the journal, pause/binding list, controls and diagnostics. Read every nearby station, move the camera and exercise mouse/controller navigation. Failure: unreadable signs, clipped non-scrollable text, overlapping panels or controls that cannot be reached. Automated rendered captures cover the three sizes plus long titles/descriptions and 20 extra temporary entries; final hardware, localization/font and tester readability acceptance remain manual.
