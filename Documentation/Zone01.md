# Zone 01 development and acceptance

Open **Tools > Windrunner > Zone 01 > Open world**. Press Play for a fresh in-memory playthrough. Stop and play again to reset. Use **Tools > Windrunner > Open Main Menu** for persistent slot testing and create a separately named playthrough. New Game creates a `zone-01` slot. Existing `starting-world` slots continue to use the previous scene and catalog. Both restore at their authored safe arrival with transient abilities cleared.

## Integration baseline

The dedicated branch starts at main-menu candidate d74cbc03. Its history already includes progression checkpoint 9e8f97c4 and production hardening aa9e9d58. Movement Lab checkpoint 0fab74bd branches separately from 9e8f97c4. The first zone uses production Sling targets, enemy health, character input, camera, portable animation, scene-local session services and the existing quest/cinematic authorities. Wind Tunnels and experimental Overdrive stay on the lab branch for a separately tested integration when a level needs them.

## Editing the zone

1. Expand **WorldRoot > Terrain**. The basin is one 2,048 × 2,048 m Unity Terrain with a 1,025 × 1,025 heightmap (2 m sample spacing), 480 m vertical range and 512 × 512 paint map. One tile keeps this initial basin continuous. Use the Terrain Inspector's **Paint Terrain** tools to raise/lower, smooth or paint. Add Terrain Layers, trees and details when ready. The data lives in **World/Terrain/CentralBasin.asset**. A duplicate scene still shares this data unless you duplicate and reassign the TerrainData on both Terrain and TerrainCollider.
2. Use the **Zone 01** selection commands to frame the arrival, contact, encounter or cache in Scene view. Move **PlayerSpawn** just above walkable ground. The session uses its position and rotation for both arrivals and loads. Move **Characters/Waykeeper** to relocate interaction and delivery together. Replace its child capsule with a model and collider. Keep the contact component on its parent. Adjust interaction range and greeting there. Move the separate clearing camera and beacon staging to reframe the shot after moving the contact.
3. Open **Tools > Windrunner > Progression Studio** and select **World/Zone01Catalog.asset**. Edit chapter names, objective descriptions, area clues, relationship ranks, rewards and Timeline references there. The optional initial rank label supplies the starting rank. The two saved chapter advances display as ranks 2 and 3. Keep released stable IDs unchanged for saved playthroughs.
4. Under **QuestRegions/North Basin**, move, rotate or resize the BoxCollider using Edit Collider. The wireframe shows the actual qualifying volume. Move **Enemies/Basin creature** roots separately to match. Their existing Sling health controls health, despawn delay and respawn delay. The enemy adapter binds each spawn to the specific player, enemy definition and region. To add a spawn, duplicate a creature and assign a unique stable ID. Quest one's objective filters the death by type, region and per-life occurrence. Its count starts at three. Leave room above and between enemies for targeting and impact. The twin pillars mark the basin entrance.
5. Move **Collectibles/Ridge cache** to change the pickup location and move the east spire if guidance changes. The child model owns the clickable collider. The pickup and NPC explicitly reference each other. The delivery objective's **Maximum Seconds** is the duration. **Respawn Seconds** on the cache controls recovery. **Allow Input** enables reticle interaction. Enable **Allow Traversal** for collection on entering its range. The clock uses scaled game time. Pause, the journal and cinematics freeze it; hit-stop uses its actual slowdown factor. A successful delivery records a single fact, then the contact uses the ordinary quest turn-in. If saving succeeds between those operations, the player can still turn in a ready quest.
6. Replace the two assets under **World/Cinematics** through the chapter cutscene definitions. Assign scene bindings on **CutsceneStaging/Milestone director** for tracks on the replacement Timelines. Keep gameplay and cinematic cameras distinct. The production cinematic safety adapter requires the player to be grounded, stationary and free of active abilities. Pending milestones can be resumed with the NPC or journal.
7. Use **Tools > Windrunner > Zone 01 > Validate open zone** after moving objects. Run Edit Mode tests for the full serialized-reference audit. Commit the scene, modified TerrainData, authored definitions and associated metadata together. The scene opens directly from disk; the batch-only initial composition command refuses an existing output tree. Nothing regenerates on load or Play.

## Manual acceptance

Use current binding labels on screen and under Settings > Controls. The defaults are E/gamepad west for Interact and J/gamepad Select for Journal. These checks need a person with a rendered Game view or Windows player.

1. **Arrival and traversal.** Start a new named playthrough from Main Menu. Look around the clearing and move, jump, reverse, dash and cast. Head beyond the basin toward the outer slopes. Check terrain collision, camera tracking, readability at speed and movement feel against the accepted player. Falling through, visible terrain gaps, a stuck camera or a lightweight substitute controller fails.
2. **Character and journal.** Approach the amber Waykeeper near the clearing. Aim the center reticle at the capsule and use Interact. Check the quest title, count, general North Basin clue, tracked HUD and journal. Walk out of range and try again. A remote interaction, unexplained exact route, repeated acceptance or premature second chapter fails.
3. **Hunt.** Go through the twin pillars. Jump and target a basin creature with Air Sling (default right mouse or gamepad right shoulder). Charge and release onto it. Repeat three qualifying kills. Check death presentation, rewards, retrievable targeting after respawn and the counter. A nonlethal hit or two callbacks for one death must never grant two kills. For an outside-region check, move one creature just outside the box in a disposable scene copy before Play.
4. **First milestone.** Return to the clearing, stand still, aim at the contact and turn in. Check the rank notification, staged beacon and shot. Skip the Timeline. Camera and movement must return immediately. If turn-in occurred while moving, stop and interact to view the pending milestone. Chapter two must remain locked until the first film is watched or skipped.
5. **Recovery.** Accept the retrieval. Travel to the single east spire. No countdown should run during the outbound trip. Collect the blue stone. Check carrying feedback. Open and close the journal and pause menu; the displayed time should hold. Let the timer expire. The quest stays active, the carried stone disappears and the cache returns after its configured delay.
6. **Load during carry.** Collect again, save, return to Main Menu and load the same slot. Expect a safe clearing arrival, rank 2, an active retrieval, no carried stone and an available cache. Check the first chapter and its cinematic record. In direct scene Play the session is intentionally temporary; use a named menu slot for this step.
7. **Final delivery.** Collect again and deliver to the Waykeeper before expiry. Check immediate countdown removal. Watch the second film to its end, replay from the journal and inspect rank 3, two completed chapters and two milestone unlocks. Save, return to title and continue. Repeated interaction or replay must never award another rank or reward. The title gallery can show unlocks; video replay there requires an authored gallery video.
8. **Editing round trip.** In a working copy, sculpt a small mound, move the cache and save. Close and reopen the scene, enter Play and return to Edit Mode. The mound and cache placement must survive. Evaluate landmark visibility, encounter spacing, retrieval time, UI overlap and the camera shot on the intended display and controller before approving the blockout.
